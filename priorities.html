Platform

Priorities for Bennington schools

Practical goals for students, taxpayers, families, and staff.

1

Put teachers first

Academic excellence happens when teachers have clear opportunities to grow. I will support those opportunities so teachers can develop their craft and provide that excellence for every student.

How I’ll implement it: Proactively schedule and foster conversations with staff, and encourage their engagement as a partnership—listen before major instructional votes, and work with teachers on the time, training, and tools that help them grow.

2

Prepare every student

I want every Bennington student prepared for college, careers, the trades, or whatever path they choose—not just better test scores on paper.

How I’ll implement it: Reduce unmanaged classroom screen time; protect recess; and expand partnerships with Metro Community College and local employers for internships, apprenticeships, and jobs.

3

Pay as we grow

Bennington is growing fast, and we need facilities and staffing that match that growth in a way families can carry. I will encourage fiscal responsibility through thoughtful discussion, aiming for best-in-class results with responsible spending. See the debt clock.

How I’ll implement it: Looking for reductions, consolidations, or delays before new spending; pairing new investments with paying down debt; inviting competitive multi-source bids on major purchases and projects; and keeping clear, plain-language tracking of debt, interest, and levy impacts so the community can see the real trade-offs.

Public record: My principle, not a formal district rule. Context: ~$159.4M outstanding debt (Sept 2025), +$112M HS#2 bond (~$12.6M reserves to construction), ~$140M budget / ~$42M tax ask at $1.358 levy, ~$310M in bonds authorized since 2003. Over that period, new debt and spending have grown faster than principal retired.

4

Follow board meetings from home

If we want the community to be engaged, we can’t treat a half-audible livestream as transparency — make board meetings easy to follow from home, and Bennington will show up.

Live-streaming was a good step. The mics feed to the ceiling speakers, and that works if you are in the room. On YouTube the camera hears those speakers, so viewers hear room sound instead of a clean voice. Home viewers also cannot see the slides.

How I’ll implement it: The fix is to feed those same mics into a mix board so the camera gets the voice directly. I will work with the school on technology solutions.

A note on process

How board members actually govern

Board members don’t run schools day to day. They set direction, adopt policy, oversee the budget, and evaluate the superintendent. If elected, I’ll ask thoughtful questions, collaborate, and keep students at the center.
